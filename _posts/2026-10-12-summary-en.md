### [Sliced Wasserstein Embeddings Power Sub-Millisecond Color-Scheme Search Over 48k Paintings](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1x39uu6/sliced_wasserstein_embeddings_hnsw_for/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The &\#x27;Palette Atlas&\#x27; project enables approximate nearest-neighbor search over 48,695 public-domain paintings under Earth Mover&\#x27;s Distance by embedding each painting&\#x27;s 128-color palette into a 128-dimensional vector using sliced Wasserstein projections onto 8 Fibonacci-hemisphere directions in OKLab space, sorted and averaged into 16 quantiles so L1 between vectors lower-bounds the true W1. Paired with a from-scratch HNSW index \(M=16, efConstruction=200\), the system achieves recall@10 of 1.000 at 0.25 ms per query versus 14 ms for brute force, and the intrinsic dimension of the vectors is about 11. Against exact EMD on 240 queries, the sliced 8×16 L1 embedding recovers 78% of the exact top-10 overlap with results only 1.8% farther, substantially beating soft color histograms with Hellinger distance \(45%, 13.5%\) and a mean-color baseline \(3%, 161%\). The author also replaces the standard HNSW top layer with 8 k-means landmarks rather than random nodes, producing one typical painting per color family without changing the average distance-computation count \(2,558 vs 2,556\).

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Potential-Barber8658 · Oct 11, 14:39

**「Background」** Earth Mover&\#x27;s Distance \(the 1-Wasserstein distance, W1\) is the natural metric between distributions of weighted colors but is too expensive for brute-force nearest-neighbor search over tens of thousands of items \(about 6 ms per pair in this dataset\). Sliced Wasserstein methods project high-dimensional distributions onto many one-dimensional directions to obtain a tractable lower bound on W1, which can then be indexed using standard approximate-nearest-neighbor structures like HNSW \(Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs\), where intrinsic dimensionality governs whether the graph hierarchy actually helps.

**「Impact」** For practitioners searching large image collections by palette similarity, the work demonstrates that a sliced-Wasserstein lower-bound embedding plus a from-scratch HNSW index can deliver exact-equivalent ANN recall over tens of thousands of items in well under a millisecond per query, with embedding quality that dominates soft-histogram and mean-color baselines against exact EMD.

### [Korean researchers use oxygen to control 2D semiconductor crystal growth](https://www.theregister.com/science/2026/10/11/researchers-work-out-how-to-control-2d-semiconductor-growth-for-future-chips/5301666) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Korean researchers have developed a method that uses oxygen to control the growth of 2D semiconductor crystals, specifically aiming to prevent crystals from forming in the wrong orientations during fabrication. The approach targets a key challenge in 2D semiconductor manufacturing, where misoriented crystal formation can degrade material quality and device performance. By introducing oxygen during the growth process, the team seeks to steer crystal alignment so that the resulting material is suitable for use in future chips. The researchers are working toward commercializing the technique by 2030, positioning it as a potential enabler for next-generation chip manufacturing. The full technical details of the method are not described in the available source snippet, so the precise mechanism and current maturity level remain unclear.

rss · The Register · Oct 11, 08:30

**「Background」** Two-dimensional semiconductors such as molybdenum disulfide are atomically thin materials under investigation as building blocks for next-generation chips, including stacked 3D architectures where multiple layers of single-crystal material are grown monolithically. Nucleation control—determining when, where, and how crystalline materials begin to form—is a fundamental challenge in semiconductor deposition, because crystals that grow in unwanted locations or with misoriented domains can degrade device performance and yield. Traditional bulk crystal growth techniques like the Czochralski method do not directly apply to 2D materials, which require new approaches to suppress stray nucleation sites and confine crystal growth to designated areas.

**「Impact」** If the approach proves scalable, it could give chip manufacturers a practical route to integrating high-quality 2D semiconductors into future devices, with commercialization targeted by 2030; the supplied source does not yet include performance data or fabrication yields to confirm practical viability.
