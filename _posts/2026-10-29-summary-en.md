### [Nvidia releases multilingual open transcription model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/voice-ai-in-vogue-nvidia-releases-new-open-transcription-model/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, a free, open-weight, promptable model for recorded speech, through Hugging Face and ModelScope. It transcribes 40 languages, translates 31 to or from English, attributes speakers in 13, handles recordings up to one hour with as many as eight speakers, and supports natural-language instructions and word-level timestamps, although audio question answering is trained only in English. Nvidia reports that the model leads open-weight models on key tests, including a 5.33% average English word error rate on Hugging Face Open ASR and an 18.26% diarization error rate across 21 languages; its hybrid Mamba–Transformer mixture-of-experts design activates roughly 4 billion of 30 billion parameters per token. Nvidia also released the Granary v2 multilingual data and curation pipeline and says fine-tuning reduced word error rate by 45.8% on held-out medical speakers compared with its best prompt, while quantized versions for local RTX Spark, DGX Spark, and Jetson AGX Thor deployment remain forthcoming. The model does not support live audio, leaving real-time voice applications to rivals such as ElevenLabs; Nvidia separately claims its forthcoming NVFP4 version processed 100 hours of audio in under three minutes on one RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPU.

rss · TechCrunch · Oct 28, 15:00

**「Background」** Speech-to-text models convert recorded or live speech into text, while diarization adds speaker labels showing who spoke and when; promptable models also accept natural-language instructions for translation, formatting, and questions about the audio. Nvidia’s Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is an open-weight model in the Nemotron speech family, released alongside the Granary v2 multilingual data and curation pipeline so developers can fine-tune recognition for particular domains and accents.

**「Impact on transcription workflows」** For developers and organizations handling prerecorded audio, Nvidia’s free, open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe provides an immediately available option for multilingual transcription, translation, and speaker attribution, making it useful for meeting and medical-dictation workflows. Because it lacks live-audio support, real-time voice applications must still use dedicated streaming models from providers such as ElevenLabs or Microsoft.
