### [Linux CVE-2026-98432 Kernel Update Warning](https://telegram.me/zaihuapd/44866) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A Telegram security notice reports a high-severity use-after-free vulnerability in Linux&\#x27;s IPv4 TCP stack, tracked as CVE-2026-98432, that an unauthenticated remote attacker may exploit without user interaction. It says Anthropic published a remote-code-execution proof of concept targeting MPTCP listening ports and cites the Go security team&\#x27;s warning that, since Go 1.24, listening sockets on Linux enable MPTCP by default, potentially exposing Go services; it also claims services without MPTCP listeners may remain vulnerable to related attacks. Operators are advised to update their kernels promptly, but the post provides no patched kernel versions, original advisory, or technical analysis, leaving the precise impact and mitigation details unverified in the supplied material.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 3, 02:37

**「Background」** A use-after-free vulnerability in Linux kernel networking code occurs when memory is accessed after it has been released, potentially enabling remote code execution through crafted network traffic. Multipath TCP \(MPTCP\) allows a connection to use multiple network paths, and Go 1.24 enabled MPTCP by default for Linux listening sockets, potentially exposing affected Go services even without explicit MPTCP configuration; the cited TCP flaw may also be reachable outside MPTCP scenarios. The source does not identify patched kernel versions or specify fully reliable mitigations, so affected-version boundaries and firewall workarounds require confirmation from upstream and distribution advisories.

**「Operational impact」** Internet-facing Linux servers running Go 1.24+ services, as well as other TCP workloads, may be exposed to unauthenticated remote code execution through CVE-2026-98432 and should install a vendor-patched kernel promptly; temporary firewall rules are reported to reduce exposure but not fully prevent exploitation.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters proposed blocking traffic before exploitation with an iptables raw PREROUTING U32 drop rule, criticized Ubuntu for assigning High rather than Critical priority and delaying fixes, and noted that RHEL reportedly offers only a mitigation said to reduce rather than eliminate exposure. Others raised concerns about consumer router vendors that rarely issue kernel security updates and linked a Hacker News discussion with 842 points and 422 comments.

### [China Unveils Crewed Lunar Landing Roadmap](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/china-releases-timeline-for-lunar-landing-on-the-moon-targeting-before-2030/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

At the Seventh Manned Spaceflight Conference in Beijing, Chinese space officials, including lunar-program chief designer Wang Xiang, presented the country’s most detailed public roadmap yet for a crewed Moon landing “before 2030,” setting schedule targets rather than reporting an achieved landing. The plan calls for an uncrewed early-2027 low-Earth-orbit test of the single-stick Long March 10A with the Mengzhou spacecraft, followed by a 2028 uncrewed Long March 10 flight using a core stage and two boosters with the Lanyue lander to demonstrate lunar descent and ascent. In 2029, two Long March 10 launches would carry uncrewed Mengzhou and Lanyue separately for docking in lunar orbit, while a later dual launch with crew aboard Mengzhou is targeted for the landing mission before 2030. The architecture uses a lunar-orbit rendezvous, with crew and lander launched separately because China lacks a Saturn V-class rocket, and the roadmap currently includes no Apollo 8- or Artemis II-style crewed lunar flyby; Mengzhou would instead gain crew experience through missions to the Tiangong space station. The published schedule provides a clearer benchmark for evaluating China’s progress and competition with NASA’s 2028 lunar-landing target, although China’s historically opaque program and the tentative dates leave significant execution uncertainty.

rss · Ars Technica · Nov 2, 17:09

**「Lunar-orbit rendezvous」** China’s plan follows the lunar-orbit rendezvous concept used during Apollo: astronauts would travel aboard the Mengzhou spacecraft, dock with the separately launched Lanyue lander in lunar orbit, and then descend to the surface. Unlike Apollo’s Saturn V, which launched the crew vehicle and lander together, China proposes separate launches using two Long March 10 vehicles, with the smaller Long March 10A initially serving as an uncrewed orbital test vehicle.

**「Program impact」** The roadmap makes China’s lunar effort trackable through staged 2027–2030 milestones, allowing observers to assess Long March 10A/10, Mengzhou, and Lanyue progress against China’s pre-2030 landing goal and NASA’s 2028 target. Because the architecture requires separate crew and lander launches plus lunar-orbit rendezvous, delays in those vehicles or tests could push the landing beyond 2030.

### [LLMs Are Not Superhuman at Weight Editing](https://seangoedecke.com/llms-are-not-superhuman-at-reading-and-writing-neural-network-weights/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

rss · Sean Goedecke · Nov 3, 00:00

**「Background」** A recent paper claims that the frontier model GPT-6 Astra can inspect and directly edit neural-network weights, including identifying what games small networks were trained to play and improving a 0.8B Qwen model at mathematics. The author argues that these demonstrations look superhuman but represent narrow, interpretable tasks rather than general AI research or recursive self-improvement.

**「Solution」** The game-weight task is unusually easy: each network consumes an 8×8 grid through labeled channels such as “agent” and “moving objects,” so a researcher can take the L2 norm across each cell’s 32 first-layer weights and visually infer the game from the resulting heatmap. The author reproduces this calculation in three lines of Python and notes that even the first three games should be recognizable from player and object locations without labels. The more substantive Qwen result targeted a specific failure: the original model solved problems requiring fewer than 32k tokens but became trapped in loops on longer ones. According to the author, Astra wrote four looping and non-looping text pairs, isolated residual differences, identified influential weights, trimmed roughly 17,000 of them, and tested a patch that stopped the looping. He compares this with abliteration and argues that direct weight surgery is analogous to editing compiled binaries: impressive, but no replacement for modifying source code and recompiling or training a model normally.

**「Takeaway」** The author’s broader conclusion is that the results demonstrate a useful manipulation technique, not superhuman AI research; intelligent systems are more likely to accelerate research by improving training than by magically editing weights. His claims that competent researchers could reproduce the work and that direct editing will not materially alter AI research are reasoned judgments rather than independently demonstrated findings.
